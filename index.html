How to generate the battery report

  1. On the laptop, press Win + R, type cmd, and press Enter.
  2. Type powercfg /batteryreport and press Enter.
  3. Read the file path printed by Windows, then open battery-report.html in a browser.
  4. Find Installed batteries. Copy Design Capacity and Full Charge Capacity into the calculator above.

Note: the report is an estimate. Battery wear, temperature, recent charging and calibration can affect the reading. If possible, also test the laptop unplugged for 10–15 minutes.

BUYER SAFETY

Tips before you pay

  • Ask for the original purchase invoice and check warranty status using the serial number through official support.
  • Check for BIOS passwords, company management or organization enrollment. If the laptop is managed by an employer or school, do not buy until the seller proves it can be released.
  • Make sure the serial number on the laptop matches the invoice where possible.
  • Test charger, Wi-Fi, webcam, microphone, speakers, keyboard, touchpad and every port.
  • Do not pay an advance before inspecting the actual device. Get a receipt with model, serial number, price and date.
  • Before final payment, ask the seller to remove their accounts and let you confirm Windows setup can start normally.
